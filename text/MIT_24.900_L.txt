2. The sound producing system
-----------------------------
Sound is produced when air is set in motion.
The air supply is provided by the lungs.
The sound is in the larynx, where a set of muscles called the vocal folds (or vocal cords) are located.
The filters are the organs above the larynx: the tube of the throat between the larynx and the oral cavity, which is called the pharynx, the oral cavity, and nasa cavity. These passages are collectively known as the vocal tract.

2.1 The lungs
A certain level of air pressure is needed to keep the speech mechanism functioning steadily.

2.2 The larynx
2.3 Glottal states
Different glottal states

    - voicelessness
Air passes directly through the glottis. Any sound made with the vocal folds in this position is said to be voiceless.
Any vibration from the vocal folds being transmitted.

    - voicing
Vocal folds is not tightly closed, air passing between then causes them to vibrate, producing sounds that are said to be voiced.

    - whisper
Whispering is voiceless, but the vocal folds are adjusted so that anterior (front) portions are pulled close together.

    - Murmur
Whispery voice. Vocal folds are relaxed to allow enough air to escape to produce a simultaneous whispery effect.

4. Consonant Articulation
-------------------------
Airflow is modified in the oral cavity by the placement of the tongue and the positioning of the lips.
These modifications occur at specific places or points of articulation.

4.1 The tongue
The primary articulation organ is the tongue.
It can be raised, lowered, thrust forward or drawn back; and even rolled back.
Five areas of the tongue.
    - tip.
    - blade
    - body
    - back
    - root

4.2 Places of articulation
Places:
    - Labial - closure or near closure of the lips. or upper teeth are called labiodentals. => p b m  f/v
    - Dental - if tongue is placed between the teeth, the sound is said to be interdental.  th
    - Alveolar - oral cavity, a small ridge protrudes from just behind the upper front teeth. => t,d,s,z,l,n
    - Alveopalatal and palatal - the roof of the mouth rises sharply. sh sure ch j
    - Velar - rear roof of the mouth.  ng, g, w
