5. Digital Modulation and Detection
-----------------------------------
Digital modulation offers a number of advantages over analog modulation, including higher spectral efficiency, powerful error correction techniques, resistance to channel impariments, more efficient multiple access strategies, and better security and privacy.

Pulse amplitude modulation              - PAM
phase-shift keying                      - PSK
quadrature amplitude modulation         - QAM
frequency-shift keying                  - FSK
minimum-shift keying                    - MSK
